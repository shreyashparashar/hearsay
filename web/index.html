              What the simulator remembers

              Real public reactions to launches, policies, crises, wars and market shocks. Every number is a hand-coded estimate on a common scale; the simulator compares each new event with these and borrows their timing and patterns.

              Patterns across events

              Hearsay simulates; it doesn't predict. Its numbers mean most when you compare options against each other.
